Emergency Mental Health Service Accessibility in the U.S.

How many psychiatric emergency walk-in services and crisis intervention teams can people reach within a 15- or 30-minute drive, and where are the gaps?

What drives access (MGWR)

Results

  • Within 15 minutes, the median county reaches fewer than one facility (0.53). Access is concentrated in metro areas.
  • At 30 minutes the median rises to 1.62, but large rural areas remain underserved, especially for walk-in services.
  • Population density and education are the variables most associated with better access, and their effects vary from place to place.

Data

  • Facilities: SAMHSA N-SUMHSS (2025), geocoded
  • Travel time: 15- and 30-minute drive-time isochrones from every census block group (Mapbox Isochrone API)
  • County variables: ACS 2019–2023 5-year estimates
