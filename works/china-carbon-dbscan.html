China's Per-Capita Carbon Emissions: Spatial Clustering

Where are China's per-capita carbon emissions concentrated, and which regions share similar emission profiles? A spatial analysis of 2020 emissions data, from global and local spatial autocorrelation to DBSCAN clustering.

Results

  • Emissions are clearly clustered: global Moran's I = 0.36 (p = 0.001).
  • High–high clusters sit in the north and northwest, and low–low clusters in the southwest.
  • DBSCAN groups regions with similar emission profiles, which points toward region-specific reduction policies.

Data & Tools

  • Data: 2020 carbon emissions by administrative unit (per-capita, agricultural, industrial, and service-sector)
  • Methods: Queen contiguity weights, global Moran's I, LISA, DBSCAN
  • Tools: Python (GeoPandas, PySAL/esda, scikit-learn)
