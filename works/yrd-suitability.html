Residential Suitability in the Yangtze River Delta

Which parts of the Yangtze River Delta are best suited for living? A county-level suitability evaluation combining water, roads, and vegetation with the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) and ArcGIS weighted overlay. Team project with Yue Chen.

Results

  • The region is broadly livable: medium and high suitability cover about 90% of the area.
  • Highest suitability is in the center (e.g., Wuzhong District, Cixi), with dense transport and water networks and good vegetation.
  • Lowest suitability is along the coast and borders (e.g., Chongming, Dingyuan), limited by poor transport access or rugged terrain.

Data & Methods

  • Data: county boundaries, major rivers, major roads, and vegetation type (Resource and Environment Science Data Center, CAS)
  • Methods: AHP weights (YAAHP), then clip, reclassify, and weighted sum in ArcGIS
