Ecological Monitoring of Fengxian District with Remote Sensing

How healthy is the ecological environment of Fengxian District, one of Shanghai's five new towns? A remote sensing assessment using Sentinel-2 and Landsat 8 imagery, image classification, and the Remote Sensing Ecological Index (RSEI).

Workflow

  • Preprocessing: Sentinel-2 10 m multiband compositing, mosaicking, calibration, and clipping; Landsat 8 thermal bands
  • Classification: compared supervised and unsupervised classification of land cover
  • Ecological indicators: greenness (NDVI), wetness, dryness (NDBSI), and heat (land surface temperature from Landsat 8)
  • RSEI: normalized the four indicators, combined them with principal component analysis, and mapped the final index

Data & Tools

  • Imagery: Sentinel-2A (June 2022), Landsat 8 OLI/TIRS
  • Tools: ENVI
